Welcome to our hive. We’re Joe and Saris, the makers behind Honey Path Designs.

We come from the Midwest and now call Colorado home. Honey Path is where our love of art, color, and unexpected little things comes together.

## A little of both of us

Saris creates our stickers, art prints, and graphic design elements. Joe makes our earrings and key chains, mostly upcycling finds from closing stores and garage sales.

Our collection brings those two creative worlds together: original illustrations and wearable pieces with a little personality.
