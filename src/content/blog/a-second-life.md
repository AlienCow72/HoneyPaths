Some of our pieces start somewhere you might not expect: a closing store or a garage sale.

Joe creates earrings and key chains, mostly from upcycled materials. Those finds become part of the Honey Path collection, alongside Saris’ original stickers and art prints.

## Little things with personality

Our earrings include guitar picks, miniature bottles, animals, and upcycled pieces. Each collection has its own kind of personality.
